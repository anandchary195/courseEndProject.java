About CollegeRide

CollegeRide is a platform designed to help college students share rides between campus and home, promoting cost-effective and eco-friendly commuting.

Key Features:

  • ✓ Easy ride posting and searching
  • ✓ Secure contact information sharing
  • ✓ Fair fare splitting among passengers
  • ✓ Flexible waiting times and schedules
  • ✓ Branch/Department-based filtering
  • ✓ Real-time ride availability

How It Works:

  1. Riders: Post your upcoming commute with destination, time, available seats, and fare
  2. Passengers: Search for rides matching your time, location, and budget
  3. Connect: View rider details and coordinate the pickup
  4. Share: Split the fare fairly among all passengers
